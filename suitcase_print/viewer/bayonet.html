Why a T-lock

Part 7's two halves meet on one straight centre line: floor, end wall and bottom wall alike. Plain keys would keep them level but let them pull apart. The T-lock's head hooks behind the lips of the slot, so once slid home the seam is held shut. Everything is inside the 5.7 mm floor; nothing shows above or below.
