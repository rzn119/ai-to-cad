Part 6 to part 7

Tongue and groove on the edges

A 1.6 mm tongue, 3 mm tall, runs along part 6's top edge all the way round, through the corners and both handle recesses (traced from your model). Part 7's bottom edge has a 2.0 × 3.4 mm groove: 0.2 mm per side. It keeps the halves in line and stops them sliding.

Print

  1. Fit test first (fit_test.stl). Adjust CLEAR_RAIL or the groove width if a fit is tight or loose, and rebuild.
  2. One piece per plate from exports/STL, already upright and placed.
  3. Tree supports under the handle recesses only: P6-BR, P7-BR, P6-E, P7-E.
  4. 5–8 mm brim on the thin straight pieces; keep elephant-foot compensation on so part 7's groove stays open.
  5. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, PLA or PETG.

Assemble

  1. Part 6: stand a piece up and slide the next one down onto its rail. Work round the U.
  2. Part 7: lower each piece past its neighbours and onto part 6's tongue.
  3. Optional: CA glue or epoxy in the slots and groove makes it permanent.
