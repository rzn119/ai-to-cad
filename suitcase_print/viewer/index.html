Within part 6: all 8 seams

Sliding T-rails

A T-rail (1.2 mm neck, 2.0 mm head, 3.0 mm out) runs up one piece's cut face and slides into a T-slot in the next, inside the 4.46 mm wall. The pieces slide together vertically, and pulled apart a seam catches after 0.25 mm.

Within part 7: the 6 side-wall seams

Tabs in the cut faces

Two tabs (1.6 × 12 mm, 3 mm out) per seam, in pockets in the next piece. Every joint inside a half of part 7 points along the side wall, so each piece goes on with one push. The centre seam is held by the bayonets.

Part 7 top → part 6 bottom

Keys and pockets

Keys 10 × 1.6 × 4 mm stand on part 7's top edge and drop into pockets 10.4 × 2.0 × 4.4 mm in part 6's bottom edge. Your seams in the two parts are 18 mm apart, so every part 6 piece bridges a part 7 seam and its keys lock the two pieces below together.

Part 7 floor

Floor keys

Tabs 12 × 2.0 mm stand 3 mm out of one edge, in the middle of the 5.7 mm floor, into pockets 12.4 × 2.4 × 3.4 mm: between floor pieces, from the bottom wall into the floor, and from the end wall into the end panel.

Part 7 centre seam

Bayonet T-locks

A T-tab (1.6 mm neck, 3.2 mm head) enters a window, then the right half slides 16 mm and the tab runs into its T-slot. The two halves of the floor cannot then be pulled apart.

Print

  1. One piece per plate from exports/v3/STL, already oriented; two strips are turned 45°.
  2. Part 6 upside down (P6-B: 14 cm³ of support instead of 510).
  3. Tree supports for the handle recesses and wheel housings.
  4. Floor panels flat, no supports.

Assemble

  1. Each half of part 7, every push towards the open end: B, then S1 onto it, F2 onto S1's floor, S2 onto S1, F3 onto F2, C onto S2 and F3.
  2. Set the right half 16 mm towards the end wall, push the halves together, slide it 16 mm towards the open end.
  3. Lower part 6 onto part 7's keys and down each other's T-rails, in this order: C-R, C-L, S2-L, S2-R, B-L, B-R, S1-L, S1-R.
