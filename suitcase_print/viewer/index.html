What I changed from your grid

Side-wall cuts

Moved to y −652 and −482. Your middle row was exactly 180.0 mm, the whole plate, and the −662.2 cut left no room for a connector next to the side-wall handle recess (y −634 to −500).

End wall: 3 pieces, not 2

Your centre cut runs through the end wall's 140 mm handle recess, the place the case is lifted by. The end wall is now cut either side of it (x 12948 and 13124.7). That makes 7 pieces per part; the handle piece fits turned 45°.

Part 7 stacks on part 6

Its bottom edge sits on part 6's top edge, which completes the two handle recesses that cross that joint. Part 6 prints upside down, part 7 upright, so the joining edge is on the bed for every piece.

Print

  1. Fit test first (fit_test.stl). If 0.20 is tight or loose, change CLEAR_KEY / CLEAR_DOWEL and rebuild.
  2. One piece per plate from exports/STL; they are already in print orientation.
  3. Tree supports only under the handle recesses: P6-BR, P7-BR, P6-E, P7-E.
  4. 5 mm brim on the thin A and B pieces. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, PLA or PETG.
  5. Keys and dowels: keys_and_dowels_plate.3mf, flat, no supports.

Assemble

  1. Stand the part 6 pieces in place (names are engraved inside).
  2. Push the dowels into the holes along part 6's top edge.
  3. Lower the part 7 pieces on: dowels into holes, seams in line.
  4. From inside, slide a key up each of the 6 seam grooves from the bottom edge until it stops.
  5. Fit the hard panel into the bottom of part 6; it also holds the keys in. A drop of CA glue makes it permanent.
